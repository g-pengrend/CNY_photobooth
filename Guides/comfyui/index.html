Steps 1 & 2

Set Your Frame Style & Copy the AI Prompt

ComfyUI Workflow Tab

💡 Click image to zoom in full screen

1 Step 1: Set Your Frame Idea

• Have an idea? Type how you want your frame to look inside the Type Frame Style & Visual Effect box.
• Don't have an idea? Just click the Re-sync from Active Theme button to automatically load the style from your active theme!

2 Step 2: Copy Prompt for AI

Click the yellow Copy Prompt for AI button, then paste the prompt into any Generative AI of your choosing (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or DeepSeek) and send it.

Steps 3 & 4

Quick Paste AI Output & Save Live

1-Click Import & Publish

💡 Click image to zoom in full screen

3 Step 3: Quick Paste AI Output

Copy the response your Generative AI gave you, click the green ⚡ Quick Paste Entire AI Output button, and paste it in. Blocks 1 to 5 (Frame, Titles, Badges, and Lighting) will fill in automatically!

4 Step 4: Save & Publish Live

Once all blocks are filled in, click the purple Save & Publish Live button at the top right to save your changes and make it live!
